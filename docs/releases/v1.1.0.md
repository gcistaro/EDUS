## v1.1.0 — Energy balance and velocity fix

This version adds the calculation of the energy and of the work done by the field. While implementing it, we found that the velocity did not allow the total energy to be conserved: this bug is now fixed.

### New features

- **Energy balance**: the new output file `Energy.txt` contains, at each time step:
  - the band energy, E_band = Tr[H0 (ρ − ρ0)];
  - the mean-field energy, E_MF = ½ Tr[Σ[ρ − ρ0] (ρ − ρ0)];
  - the total energy, E = E_band + E_MF;
  - the power P = −E(t)·v(t) and the work of the field, W(t) = ∫ P dt.

  Without decay, the energy of the electrons changes only because of the work of the field: E(t) − E(0) = W(t).
- **Energy check in the CI**: `ci-test/check_energy.py` verifies E(t) − E(0) = W(t) in all tests without decay.
- **Utilities**:
  - `Postproces/hermitize_tb.py` makes a Wannier90 `seedname_tb.dat` file exactly Hermitian;
  - `Postproces/Velocity_fft.py` computes the Fourier transform of the velocity.

### Bug fix: velocity and energy conservation

- In the commutator [r, H] of the velocity operator only H0 was used, and the mean-field self energy Σ was never included. Without Σ the total energy is not conserved.
- The velocity is now computed with the same Hamiltonian that propagates the density matrix:
  v = ⟨∇k(H0 + Σ) + i[H0 + Σ, r]⟩.

### Changes in the results

- **Velocity and absorption spectra change** with respect to v1.0.x in calculations beyond IPA (RPA, HSEX), where Σ ≠ 0. In IPA Σ = 0, so the velocity is unchanged.
- The **positions of the absorption peaks never change**, in any of the tests: the results are consistent.
- What changes is the **oscillator strength** of the peaks, although not significantly.
- The reference outputs of the tests have been updated accordingly.
